How it works
  1. Lay two rows of cards face up on a plain, light mat in soft side light. Tap Start a scan.
  2. Glide your phone slowly along each row. Up to 30 cards per scan. I pick out each card for you.
  3. Your cards land in your Binder. Name them, sort them, and keep notes.
Clearing scans does not touch your Binder. Your Binder keeps its own copies.

✅4 quick tips for clean photos
• Tilt the phone slightly, so shine slides off the card.
• Avoid light straight overhead. It makes glare and shadows.
• Soft light from one side works best.
• Lay cards on a plain, light mat or a sheet of paper.
🎞️Row video scan: lay 2 rows flat, fit the first card inside the frame (it should fill it), tap Start sweep, then glide along the row. About 1 to 2 seconds per card. Going on to the next row? Just move down and glide back the other way, or tap Stop and Start next sweep.
🛡️Keep valuable cards in their sleeves. We can read them through the plastic. If you see shine, tilt a little so it slides off the edge.
🧻A plain, light-colored mat or sheet of paper under the cards works better than a dark or speckled counter.
💡Soft light from the side works best. Keep your phone's shadow off the cards. Up to 30 cards per scan.
